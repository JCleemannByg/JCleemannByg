J Cleemann Byg is a small workshop with high standards for the finish. There is room to
make the decisions that matter – the species of wood, the joint, the last piece of trim –
and time to make them properly.

Every job is adapted to the task and to the house. That applies whether a built-in or a shed is
being built, or a single skirting board has to look as if it was always there.
