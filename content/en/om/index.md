J Cleemann Byg is a small workshop in Nærum. Carpentry and joinery are carried out in the
craft tradition: proper preparation, considered joints and a finish that holds up when
everyday life arrives.

The approach is simple. Listen to the task, measure in reality and choose materials that
suit the house and the way it is used.
