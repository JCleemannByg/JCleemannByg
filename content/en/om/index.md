J Cleemann Byg is a small workshop in Nærum, established on 3 March 2023 as a sole
trader business. The work ranges from built-in furniture such as radiator covers and
bookcases to outdoor structures and renovation with insulation.

The approach is simple: Listen to the task, measure in reality and choose materials that
suit the house and the way it is used.

A job begins with a conversation and a survey, where materials and finish are agreed. After
that the work is carried out and finished down to the last details.
